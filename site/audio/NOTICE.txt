SpeakHarness website voice examples

Generated locally through SpeakHarness's createEngineClient().synthesize() and
encodeWav(), with speed 1.0. The example text was authored for this website;
no Claude session, customer data, microphone recording or voice clone was used.

English: kokoro:af_heart, Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX (q4), 24 kHz PCM.
Portuguese: piper:pt_BR-faber-medium, 22.05 kHz PCM.
MP3 delivery encoding: libmp3lame, 96 kbit/s, no changes to voice or timing.
Text, voice IDs, original PCM duration and 72 measured RMS waveform bins are
recorded in samples.json. Encoder padding may slightly change MP3 duration;
the web player uses the media element's actual duration after metadata loads.
